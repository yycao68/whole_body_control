# V3 Unitree G1 Benchmark Plan

## Objective

Validate a dual interaction-dynamics architecture:

1. Level 1: finite-horizon centroidal double-integrator MPC.
2. Level 2: instantaneous whole-body inverse-dynamics interface.
3. Level 3: finite-horizon task double-integrator MPC.

Both MPCs optimize residual acceleration and use exact ZOH models. The
benchmark must distinguish prediction, offset estimation, contact recovery,
and cross-port anticipation.

## Platform

- Plant: local Menagerie-derived Unitree G1 MJCF.
- Physics: 0.5 ms.
- Task and physical interface: 1 ms.
- Initial Level-1 target: 5-10 ms, reduced only after timing verification.
- Primary actuation: torque actuators in a hardware-parity MJCF.
- Position-as-torque version: reported only as an ablation.

## G1 Walking Visualization Gate

Before the full torque-QP walking benchmark, the repository includes a
deterministic 10 s dual-MPC root-assisted Unitree G1 walking visualization:

```bash
MPLCONFIGDIR=/private/tmp/mplconfig python3 whole_body_control/versions/v3/code/run_g1_root_assist_demo.py
```

This run follows the `g1_ab_simulation` scaffold for visual stability, but the
body reference is generated by the normalized centroidal interaction MPC and
the right-hand motion is regulated by the normalized task interaction MPC. The
floating base remains kinematically assisted, the G1 executes alternating
one-foot swing commands, and the stance foot is lightly pinned for a stable
visual artifact.

The current deterministic no-push result uses a trapezoidal speed command:
0--1 s ramp from rest to 1.2 m/s, 1--9 s cruise at 1.2 m/s, and 9--10 s
deceleration to rest. It produces a 10.0 s duration, 10.800 m forward distance,
0.083/0.083 m left/right foot lift, 0.752 m minimum CoM height, 0.030 rad
maximum roll/pitch magnitude, 95.5 mm hand RMS error, 15 support switches, and
no fall. The generated summary, log, plot, and video are stored in
`code/results/`.

This gate is useful for checking the G1 model, video pipeline, MPC command-layer
integration, visible foot lifting, and 10.8 m of forward walking. It is
not a substitute for S4, because the final S4 claim requires torque actuation,
contact-wrench recovery through the inverse-dynamics QP, and randomized push
trials.

## Torque-Actuated Realizer Smoke Gate

The repository now includes an initial torque-actuated runner:

```bash
MPLCONFIGDIR=/private/tmp/mplconfig python3 whole_body_control/versions/v3/code/run_g1_torque_realizer_benchmark.py --scenario stand --duration 3.0 --trials 1 --seed 31
MPLCONFIGDIR=/private/tmp/mplconfig python3 whole_body_control/versions/v3/code/run_g1_torque_realizer_benchmark.py --scenario stand_push --duration 3.0 --trials 3 --seed 21
MPLCONFIGDIR=/private/tmp/mplconfig python3 whole_body_control/versions/v3/code/run_g1_torque_realizer_benchmark.py --scenario contact_switch --duration 3.0 --trials 1 --seed 41
MPLCONFIGDIR=/private/tmp/mplconfig python3 whole_body_control/versions/v3/code/run_g1_torque_realizer_benchmark.py --scenario walk --duration 3.0 --trials 1 --seed 51
```

It generates a local torque-actuated `models/g1_wbc_torque.xml`, runs the
normalized body/task MPCs with the Kalman-style random-walk disturbance
observer, applies a present-sample inverse-dynamics/contact QP realizer, and
logs pushes, detected contact events, torque-limit utilization, post-QP
clipping residual, friction margin, QP residuals, falls, and realization
failures.

Current result: the fixed-support portion now passes, but the walking portion
does not. The no-push standing trial completes 3.0 s without falling, and the
three randomized-push standing trials all complete 3.0 s while detecting every
injected push. After the benchmark was aligned with the faithful CoM-acceleration
recovery used in H2 and a DCM lateral-sway stepping reference, the contact-switch
trial carries five contact-mode switches before falling at 2.041 s, and the
walking trial carries eight switches before falling at 1.889 s. Both failures
occur with active torque limits, nonzero QP residual/fallback samples, and
negative measured foot-floor friction margins; the dominant observed failure
mode remains single-support balance authority rather than the old centered-CoM
scaffold. Therefore the root-assisted walking video has not been replaced by a
valid torque-level walking result. The next required implementation is a
production gait/contact realizer with hip/angular-momentum balance,
capture-point step timing/placement adaptation, and stable support-mode control.

## S3 Acceptance Gate

The foot-transition experiment is valid only when:

- swing-foot clearance is at least 30 mm;
- swing foot is contact-free for at least 95% of dwell;
- stance foot remains within friction and CoP bounds;
- mode detection does not read the gait schedule;
- at least 90% of planned cycles complete without falling.

Otherwise use the hand-brace transition and name it accordingly.

## Logged Signals

- full state and reference;
- both normalized MPC states, controls, and disturbances;
- desired and recovered centroidal/task wrenches;
- per-contact force, friction margin, and CoP;
- whole-body QP residuals and slacks;
- joint torque/position/velocity limits;
- assumed, detected, scheduled, and MuJoCo ground-truth contact modes;
- construction, solve, and total controller time.

## Statistical Reporting

- Publish raw CSV/NPZ logs and seeds.
- Use paired trials across controllers.
- Report median, interquartile range, and bootstrap 95% confidence intervals.
- Include falls and infeasible solves in denominators.
- Freeze tuning before randomized evaluation.
